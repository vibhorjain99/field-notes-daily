The Foundational Shift · 2022–2026

From statistical parrots to reasoning systems: how four years rebuilt every assumption you brought from 2021.

If you put down ML in 2021, your mental model is roughly: CNNs for vision, BERT for text, RL is mostly games. Four years later, a single architecture — the decoder-only transformer, scaled with RLHF and chain-of-thought — has eaten most of that. The question stopped being "which model" and became "which prompt, which tools, which agent loop."

This dashboard is built around five inflection points: InstructGPT (2022) aligning LLMs with human preference, ChatGPT (Nov 2022) proving the interface, GPT-4 + multimodality (2023), reasoning models like o1/DeepSeek-R1 (late 2024–2025) trading latency for thought, and agentic systems (2025–2026) that act, not just answer.
